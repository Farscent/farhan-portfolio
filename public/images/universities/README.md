# University artwork

The Education section uses Farhan's supplied artwork:

- UGM_logo.png: copied unchanged from the supplied transparent PNG.
- NTU.png: supplied NTU coat of arms, saved as PNG with identical decoded pixels.

Original colors and proportions are preserved with object-fit: contain. Logos are centered in transparent containers without white backgrounds or borders. On mobile each logo spans the programme and academic-details rows.

Update paths and alternative text in src/data/education.ts to replace these images. University marks remain the property of their respective institutions.
