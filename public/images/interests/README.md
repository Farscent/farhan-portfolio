# Original hobby illustrations

These SVGs were created for this portfolio and are conceptual editorial illustrations.

- `anime-worldbuilding.svg`: imagined Japanese night skyline, traditional shrine, torii gate, moon, and layered architecture. No copyrighted characters or third-party artwork.
- `motorsport-engineering.svg`: imagined silver and black open-wheel racing car, aerodynamic bodywork, exposed tires, and teal airflow linework. No official team branding or telemetry results.

Both have a 1200 × 900 viewBox, scale responsively, and retain their full composition at a 4:3 aspect ratio. Accessible descriptions are provided by the website component.
